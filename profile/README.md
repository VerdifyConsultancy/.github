# Verdify

**Verified AI operations for high-trust workflows.**

Verdify helps teams put AI agents into operational workflows without giving up control. We design and build bounded AI systems where the agent role, allowed actions, approval points, authoritative systems, telemetry, and scorecard are explicit before the workflow expands.

This GitHub organization hosts selected public work behind Verdify's consulting practice and live proof environment: lab software, control-loop examples, operating documentation, and tools that make AI workflows easier to inspect.

## What We Do

- **Verified AI Operations Audit:** map a real workflow, identify where AI belongs, define controls, and decide what should be built first.
- **Controlled AI MVP Sprint:** implement one narrow workflow path with approvals, logging, exception handling, and acceptance criteria.
- **AI Operations Scorecard:** define the metrics, evaluation rubric, review cadence, and evidence needed to decide whether a workflow should expand.
- **AgentOps Retainer:** monitor, tune, review incidents, report outcomes, and expand only when evidence supports it.

## How We Work

The Verdify method is intentionally practical:

1. **Map** the workflow, systems, people, decisions, exceptions, and current pain.
2. **Define controls** for what AI may read, draft, recommend, execute, or never touch.
3. **Build** one narrow path with controlled integrations and clear acceptance criteria.
4. **Verify** quality, exceptions, telemetry, reviewer signal, and operational impact.
5. **Operate** with scorecards, incident review, known limits, and expansion gates.

## Where It Applies

Verdify is built for repeated, reviewable, measurable work where wrong AI actions would matter. Common starting points include:

- Support ticket triage and escalation prep
- Procurement and security response packets
- Quality documentation and evidence assembly
- Field-service exception triage
- Retailer claims, chargebacks, and compliance packets
- Regulated document review and traceability workflows
- Telemetry and sensor anomaly synthesis
- Internal knowledge and SOP retrieval

## Proof Posture

Verdify Lab is our public proof environment: a real greenhouse in Longmont, Colorado where an AI planning agent proposes tactics, control layers constrain writes, firmware owns physical action, and telemetry verifies what happened.

The lab is not a greenhouse product. It is a public operating pattern for the same discipline Verdify brings to client workflows: explicit authority, bounded action, logs, scorecards, caveats, and known limits.

## Trust Principles

- Humans, deterministic controls, and systems of record remain authoritative where consequences matter.
- AI may propose, draft, classify, route, or recommend only inside approved boundaries.
- Irreversible, regulated, unsafe, or customer-facing actions require explicit controls and accountable approval.
- Sensitive data paths are scoped, minimized, and reviewed before implementation.
- Impact claims need scorecard evidence, not adoption enthusiasm.
