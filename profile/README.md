# Verdify

**Turn enterprise expertise into AI systems that get better at your work.**

Colorado-based Verdify helps organizations turn human expertise into an AI learning loop they can own, measure, and improve. Your people know where work breaks and what good judgment looks like. Verdify turns that expertise into source-grounded, controlled AI workflows whose feedback and evidence stay with your organization.

AI models, and the vendors that provide them, can be replaced. Your organization-owned learning loop remains the advantage.

This GitHub organization hosts selected public work behind Verdify's consulting practice and its Public Proof Lab. The current wording, services and proof live at [verdify.ai](https://verdify.ai/).

## Services

Start at the earliest unresolved question, and move forward only when the evidence supports the next stage.

- **[AI Learning Loop Audit](https://verdify.ai/services/verified-ai-operations-audit/):** map the workflow, expertise, source knowledge, authority, risks, feedback, and outcomes before committing to an implementation path.
- **[Knowledge Architecture](https://verdify.ai/knowledge/):** organize approved sources, owners, access rules, freshness, citations, and gaps so people and AI can work from a source layer the organization controls.
- **[Agentic Workflow Design Sprint](https://verdify.ai/services/verified-ai-mvp-sprint/):** build a narrow workflow where AI can read, classify, summarize, draft, recommend, or route while human approval and system authority stay explicit.
- **[Private Evals and Outcome Scorecard](https://verdify.ai/services/ai-operations-scorecard/):** define test cases, reviewer rubrics, operating metrics, known limits, and the evidence required for an expand, tune, hold, or stop decision.
- **[Feedback Loop Operations](https://verdify.ai/services/agentops-retainer/):** review exceptions, overrides, incidents, feedback, eval results, and outcomes, and expand authority only when the record supports it.

## The Verdify Method

Own the learning loop. Keep the AI model and its provider replaceable.

1. **Map expertise:** find the sources, judgment, owners, exceptions, and outcomes that make the workflow valuable.
2. **Architect the loop:** separate what AI may do from what people, policy, controls, and systems of record own.
3. **Build the workflow:** create one narrow path where AI assists real work and every consequential action stays traceable.
4. **Measure outcomes:** use private evals, reviewer decisions, exceptions, and operating evidence to judge performance.
5. **Compound learning:** turn corrections and results into organization-owned signal that improves the next cycle.
